## 1. Executive Overview & Architecture
The **Production Competitor & Market Price Intelligence Monitoring Engine** is a full-stack, enterprise-grade software and agency automation ecosystem engineered specifically for B2B SaaS founders, product pricing committees, digital marketing agencies, and e-commerce brands. It automates legal, RFC-compliant public website/HTML/JSON-LD pricing extraction (strictly zero authentication bypass, zero scraping of personal data, robots.txt-respecting), semantic price difference and currency normalization (USD, INR, EUR, GBP), dynamic repricing rule simulation with margin guardrails and MAP compliance, SaaS feature entitlement matrix diffing, automated executive competitor digests (Markdown/HTML/PDF), price elasticity modeling, and a high-performance Cloudflare Workers edge REST router backed by Cloudflare D1 SQL.

### System Highlights:
1. **Public Pricing & Competitor Scraper:** Extracts structured schema.org Product, Offer, and priceSpecification entities as well as public pricing tables. Completely legal, compliant, and RFC-bounded.
2. **Price Change & Anomaly Alert Generator:** Detects plan fee hikes, stealth downgrades, annual discount shifts, seat-based tier thresholds, promotional flash sales, and normalizes multi-currency pricing to USD and INR.
3. **Price Elasticity & Dynamic Repricing Modeler:** Dynamic repricing rule simulator enforcing gross margin guardrails, Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) compliance, competitive undercut/match rules, and market percentiles.
4. **SaaS Feature Matrix Comparator:** Constructs automated multi-competitor plan comparison grids, entitlement matrices, packaging strategy analyzers, and pricing ladder diffs.
5. **Executive Digest Synthesizer:** Compiles weekly and monthly strategic intelligence briefs for founders and VPs of Product highlighting market movements, hidden discounts, and packaging shifts.
6. **Cloudflare Workers Edge REST Gateway:** Serverless edge router ingesting pricing webhooks, competitor telemetry alerts, and dispatching real-time notifications to Slack, Discord, and ERP/Shopify/Amazon repricing APIs.
7. **Commercial B2B Monetization Engine:** Complete agency retainer kit with formal Master Price Intelligence Service Agreement (15-minute price polling SLA & antitrust compliance guarantee), client intake brief, white-label pitch deck, and dual-currency (USD/INR) programmatic invoicing engine compliant with Indian GST LUT zero-rated export provisions.
